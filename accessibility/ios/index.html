iOS Accessibility

Train your way, without unnecessary barriers.

FourEver is built so the essential timer workflow remains clear, operable, and understandable with iPhone accessibility features.

Accessibility release status

These improvements are undergoing final device and assistive-technology validation. App Store accessibility labels will only be enabled after every common workout task passes that review.

VoiceOver and Voice Control

  • Controls use clear names, values, hints, and native accessibility roles.
  • Workout status includes the current phase, remaining time, set, round, and what comes next.
  • Accessible actions are provided for expanding controls, unlocking them, repeating status, and jumping through a sequence.
  • Phase changes are announced without reading every timer tick or duplicating spoken phase cues.
  • Visible names and input aliases support Voice Control, Show Names, Show Numbers, and timer-name dictation.

Larger Text

FourEver supports Dynamic Type, including accessibility text sizes. Dense horizontal layouts reflow into readable vertical or wrapping layouts, while timer controls remain available with larger text.

Visual accessibility

  • Light, Dark, and System appearances are supported throughout the app.
  • Important text, icons, borders, and selected states are designed for sufficient contrast, including Increase Contrast.
  • Phase and selection states use words, symbols, outlines, and checkmarks instead of relying on color alone.
  • Reduce Transparency replaces translucent materials with more solid system backgrounds where needed.

Reduced Motion

When Reduce Motion is enabled, FourEver removes spring, scale, sliding, and continuous decorative animation where possible. Essential state changes use restrained fades or immediate transitions while direct, user-controlled gestures remain available.

Workout cues and system surfaces

Workout progress is communicated visually and can also use sound, spoken cues, and haptics. Lock Screen Live Activities and Dynamic Island views expose concise status and supported actions within the space controlled by iOS.

Languages and media

The app interface and new accessibility descriptions are available in English, German, Russian, and Ukrainian.

FourEver does not contain videos, podcasts, or narrative media. Captions and Audio Descriptions therefore do not apply to the current app content.

Accessibility feedback

If something prevents you from completing a workout task, please tell us which feature you use, what you expected, and what happened.
