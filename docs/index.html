Fourever

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Short version: Fourever is an interval timer. Your timers, preferences, and workout history are stored locally on your device. We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand aggregate app use and diagnose crashes. We do not sell personal information or show ads.

1. Information we collect

Fourever stores the timers you create, your settings, and your completed session history locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to our servers by the app.

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may collect device and app information such as app interactions, device model, operating-system version, app version, crash diagnostics, and identifiers used to help us understand app stability and aggregate usage.

If you choose to contact support or suggest a feature by email, we receive the email address, message, and any information you choose to send. The draft prepared by Fourever may include the app version, Android version, and device model to help us troubleshoot. You can review, edit, or remove this information before sending the email.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and retain your timers, settings, and session history on your device.
  • To understand aggregate feature use and improve the app.
  • To diagnose, prioritize, and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To respond to support requests and consider feature suggestions that you send us.

We deliberately do not send timer names or other user-entered text to our Firebase analytics events.

3. Sharing and service providers

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics as service providers. Their handling of information is governed by Google's policies, including the Firebase Privacy and Security information and Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information. Fourever does not contain advertising SDKs.

4. Notifications and device permissions

Fourever may request notification permission so it can display an ongoing workout notification while a timer is active. This permission is used only for the timer experience and does not give us access to your personal notifications or their contents.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can delete timers and session-history entries inside the app. Uninstalling Fourever removes the app's locally stored data from your device. Aggregated analytics and crash information may be retained by Firebase according to Google's retention practices.

Support and feature-request emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and support or improve Fourever.

6. Children

Fourever is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The current version will always be available at this page, with the updated date shown above.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email us at fourever.support@proton.me.
