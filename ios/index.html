iOS Privacy Policy

FourEver

Effective date: 26 July 2026

FourEver is an interval timer for iPhone. The short version: FourEver does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you create in the app stays strictly on your device.

What FourEver Stores

FourEver saves the following on your device only:

  • Your timers — names, intervals, rounds, sets, and phase durations.
  • Your workout history — completed and partial sessions, with the date, duration, and number of rounds.
  • Your preferences — sounds, cues, theme, and settings.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded anywhere, and there is no account required to sign in.

If you have iPhone backups enabled, this data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, in the same way as data from any other app. Those backups belong to you and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What FourEver Does Not Do

  • No analytics: Contains zero usage-tracking software.
  • No crash-reporting SDKs: No third-party crash reporters bundled.
  • No advertising: No ads, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs: FourEver has zero external code dependencies.
  • No network connections: Contains no networking code and does not send or receive data over the internet.
  • No accounts: No sign-in or email address required.
  • No microphone access: FourEver plays audio; it never records.
  • No sensitive sensors: No access to Health, Contacts, Photos, or Location data.

Permissions FourEver Asks For

Notifications: If enabled, FourEver may show a notification when a workout finishes. These are scheduled and delivered locally by your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server, and there are no push notifications.

Audio while the screen is locked: FourEver keeps playing audio in the background so the timer keeps running and you can hear cues with your phone in a pocket. This only produces sound; it never records.

You can change or withdraw either permission at any time in iOS Settings → FourEver.

Live Activities & Dynamic Island

While a workout is running, FourEver can show the current interval on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This is drawn locally on your device from data already on your device using zero remote push services.

Contacting Support

The Contact Support and Suggest a Feature buttons in Settings open a new message in your email app, pre-filled with:

  • The FourEver version you are running
  • Your iOS version
  • Your device model identifier (e.g., iPhone17,1)

This information is included so bug reports can be acted on effectively. Nothing is sent until you manually choose to send it. Messages arrive at fourever.support@proton.me, and support emails are used strictly to answer your inquiry.

Crash Reports from Apple

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide the developer with crash and performance data it has collected. This process is controlled entirely by Apple's setting, not something FourEver collects itself.

Your Data, Rights & Children

FourEver does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Because FourEver collects no personal data, there is nothing held about you to access, correct, export, or delete. Your data stays entirely under your control:

  • Clear History in Settings removes your saved sessions.
  • Reset Settings in Settings restores preferences to default without affecting timers or history.
  • Deleting the app permanently removes all timers, history, and settings from the device.
